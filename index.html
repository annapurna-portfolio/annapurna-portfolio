About

I'm a Senior UX/UI Designer with 6+ years across enterprise and consumer products in telecom, finance, and SaaS. I work end to end — research, prototyping, and usability testing — and have delivered for global clients including ThoughtWorks, Dell, Vodafone, and Renault.

Today I'm designing a Monitoring, Reporting & Verification (MRV) engine at Carbon Chain India — simplifying complex carbon datasets into intuitive, data-driven experiences for sustainability platforms. I'm an HFI Certified Usability Analyst and treat accessibility and design systems as part of the craft, not an afterthought.

Most recently I've gone hands-on with AI/ML data annotation — bounding-box image annotation in CVAT and basic object-detection model training with YOLOv8 in Google Colab.
